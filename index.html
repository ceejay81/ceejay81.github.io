About

I'm a recent BSIT graduate and full-stack developer based in General Santos City, South Cotabato. My work sits at the intersection of practical software engineering and public service — building systems that real communities depend on daily.

During my internship at the City Treasurer's Office, I collaborated on four production systems — from an appointment platform used by thousands of health office visitors, to a fuel assistance registry for tricycle drivers. I was also independently hired by a local barangay unit to build their Barangay Blotter System, replacing manual logbooks with a secure database workflow. These aren't portfolio experiments; they're live, maintained systems running on government infrastructure.

My primary stack is Laravel + PHP on the backend and Flutter for mobile. I adapt quickly and I'm drawn to problems where software creates a measurable improvement in how government services reach people.
